# Chromecast with Google TV

## Goal

Help Plex users on Chromecast with Google TV choose the playback quality that avoids unnecessary transcoding.

## Disable transcoding / set quality to original

1. Open the Plex app on Chromecast with Google TV.
2. Select the video you want to watch.
3. Start playback.
4. While the video plays, press the `Up` button on the remote to open the playback controls.
5. Select `Quality`, `Video Quality`, or `Playback Settings`.
6. Choose `Original` or the highest available quality.
7. If `Direct Play` or `Direct Stream` appears, enable it.
8. Close the menu and return to playback.

## Notes

- Chromecast with Google TV is more like an Android TV device than a classic Chromecast.
- If the file has unsupported codecs, Plex may still transcode.
