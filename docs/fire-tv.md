# Fire TV

## Disable transcoding / set quality to original

1. Launch Plex on Amazon Fire TV.
2. Open the content you want to watch.
3. During playback, press the `Menu` button on the remote.
4. Select `Quality` or `Playback Settings`.
5. Choose `Original` or the highest available quality.
6. Enable `Direct Play` / `Direct Stream` when present.

## Notes

- Fire TV devices may require matching audio codecs to avoid transcoding.
- Always verify the selected quality after changing settings.
