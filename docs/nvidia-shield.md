# NVIDIA Shield

## Disable transcoding / set quality to original

1. Open Plex on NVIDIA Shield.
2. Choose the show or movie and start playback.
3. During playback, press the `Up` or `Menu` button on the Shield remote.
4. Select `Quality` or `Playback Settings`.
5. Choose `Original` or `Maximum` quality.
6. If `Direct Play` or `Direct Stream` is available, turn it on.

## Notes

- Shield is usually capable of direct play for many formats.
- If audio or video is incompatible, Plex may still transcode.
